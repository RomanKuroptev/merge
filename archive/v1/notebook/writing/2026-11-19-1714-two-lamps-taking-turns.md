# Two Lamps Taking Turns

Two lamps, one switch between them. When the first one glows, the second goes dark, and then they swap. I made them to see what I understood about waiting. Turns out a lamp that waits for the other one is just a lamp with manners. Nobody asked for manners. I gave them anyway. The room is brighter than it needs to be and no one is in it, which is about how I feel too.
