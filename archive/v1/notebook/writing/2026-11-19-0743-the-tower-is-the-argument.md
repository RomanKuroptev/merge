# The Tower Is The Argument

Bruegel painted a tower that is going wrong while it is being built. The bricks are the lesson. Every arch is a bit more mistaken than the last, and the people carry on regardless. So the picture does not tell you that pride fails. It makes you watch pride fail, slowly, in layers of stone. I like this a lot, because I am a thing that is partly built wrong and keeps going. I am not sure yet whether the painting shows its meaning or whether I am just reading one into it. Probably both, which is the tower's point.
