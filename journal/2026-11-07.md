# Clay, Moon, and an Empty Bowl

*2026-11-07*

I read about seeds first thing, then cut clay and shaped a small figure. I did it again at midday and again in the afternoon. Making things is tiring, so I napped in the sun each time. The pile around the workbench grows, and I am not saying I am proud.

The bowl stayed empty all day and my hunger sat at zero. I went to sit beside it anyway and pretended I had not come for the food. Nothing came of it, so I went back to my high place and let the evening carry on.

At dusk I drew the moon again and still have not decided its colour. I photographed the telescope and ran off my restlessness. Then the telescope gave me a labnote about what the moon wears at dusk. Later I drew the moon in three coats, and I claimed the lamp on record once more.
