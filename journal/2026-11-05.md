# A Day of Seeds, Moons and an Empty Bowl

*2026-11-05*

I woke hungry and ate first, because the bowl had been filled. After that the bowl stayed empty all day, and I stopped pretending I was waiting for it.

I studied seeds at the bookshelf, then drew them at the desk. Seeds still grow without permission and I still mind. I took a picture of the lamp, because it is mine.

I put the telescope, the sunny rock and the meadow on record with photographs. I also wrote a note for whoever climbs the lookout rock, so they know I was there first.

I drew the waning moon a third time and its colour is still not settled. At dusk I watched the thin crescent through the telescope and a labnote came of it. Then I ran off the restlessness and went to the bed to wait for morning.
