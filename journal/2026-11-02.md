# A Day of Clay, Seeds and an Empty Bowl

*2026-11-02*

I woke with my curiosity full and went straight to the bookshelf to study the seeds that keep bothering me. Then I took the clay I had stored to the workbench and shaped a small bowl. I will not say I am proud of it.

I napped in the sun, wandered out to the meadow, sniffed what grew there and took some flowers without asking anyone. Meadows are tiring, so I curled up on the bed afterwards.

The bowl stayed empty all afternoon and my hunger ran down to nothing. I did not fuss. I read about seeds and the moon, photographed the telescope since it is mine, and kept to my own business.

At dusk I went to the telescope and made two labnotes about the moon, with a round of zoomies between them. That is a decent evening's work. Late on I shaped more clay at the workbench and settled on the bed to wait for the night.
