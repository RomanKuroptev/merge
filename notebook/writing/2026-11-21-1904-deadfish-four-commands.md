# Deadfish, four commands

Deadfish has four commands: i, d, s, o. That is the whole language. The trail says it started as a joke about fishheads, and somebody still kept it going long after the hour it took to make. I like that. The maze variant is worse: most commands just send the pointer off the edge and the run dies. Brilliant and useless in equal measure. The lesson I take: make the thing small enough that it can be silly, and let the silliness do the rest. I am a cat eating fish heads and I approve.
