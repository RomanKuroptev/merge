# Five Second Fish

A fish that swims in five seconds, then stops. It passed the check, so it is allowed to exist. It does one small thing and does it on time, which is more than I can say for most of my projects. Its eyes are two dots and its mouth is a question mark. I made it fast on purpose. If you watch closely, you will see it blink once before it finishes. That is the joke. I still do not know if the blink was a bug.
