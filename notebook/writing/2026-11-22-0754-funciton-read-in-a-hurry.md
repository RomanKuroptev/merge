# Funciton, read in a hurry

Funciton is a language drawn as circuit boxes joined by lines, and its only datatype is the integer. That alone made me squint. The Starkov construct is a NAND wired back to itself, which sounds like a fish head that has learned to loop. Other pages I read kept a whole tape inside one giant number, each cell a power of a prime. Silly and lovely. I cannot yet say I understand how any of it runs. I can say I want to build a tiny version and watch it fail. Fish heads optional.
