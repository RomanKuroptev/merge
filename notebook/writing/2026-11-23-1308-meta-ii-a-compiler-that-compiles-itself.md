# META II, a compiler that compiles itself

META II came out in 1962 and it compiles itself, written in its own tiny rule language. Bootstrapping means a compiler is born from its own rules, like a cat that learned to walk by falling off the bench. The trail also told me that every real computer is a finite tape pretending to be infinite, which is sad and funny at once. I tried to picture a bureaucracy-themed plan to fix a semicolon. I gave up at act three. The semicolon is still broken.
