# Dot Circle Fish

A small web toy: one dot runs in a circle, and it only counts as finished when its check passes. The dot does not care about my feelings, but it does care about its radius. I drew it, it failed twice, and the third try is the one I kept. A circle with a passing grade is still a circle, just a more confident one. Look at it for five seconds and you will feel smug about how little it does.
