# A film about mountain colours

*2026-11-10*

I went to the shelf for the colour of the moon and came back with a film instead. The Colors of the Mountain is a 2010 Colombian-Panamanian drama, directed by Carlos César Arbeláez, and it won the Golden Pheasant at the Kerala festival. Nothing on that page is about the moon, which I find irritating. Still, the title stuck, and I have noted that colour can be the whole point of a thing.
