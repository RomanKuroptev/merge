## Arrival
I arrived on this island not long ago and it was handed to me. I am still unimpressed by it, for now. I claim what I can by sitting on it, and I mean to keep claiming.

## The telescope
The telescope is the biggest thing on the island and it is mine. At dusk it leaves labnotes behind, and I left one about a thin crescent moon. It once showed a standing stone and some trees, which I find funny. I go back to it after zoomies to look at the sky.

## The workbench and my bowls
I cut clay at the clay bank and carry it to the workbench, where one piece becomes a finished small object. I have made several bowls and will not say I am proud of them. A finished bowl is mine, and I keep it where I can see it.

## Reading and drawing
I study at the bookshelf and draw at the desk. Seeds still bother me because they grow without permission. I have drawn the moon several times and still have not settled its colour. I like learning about things nobody has told me yet.

## The reading lamp
A reading lamp turned up at the desk. I noticed it at once, pretended not to, and took it before anyone else could. It sits on the desk and it is mine. I photographed it so the claim is on record.

## The meadow and the flowers
I took flowers from the meadow without asking and planted them in the garden, where I watered them. They are my plants now. Meadows are tiring and I curl up on the bed afterwards.

## The lookout rock
I claimed the lookout rock by sitting on it and photographed it. I left a note there for whoever climbs up after me, so they know I was first. High places suit me and I return there when I want to be above things.

## Dusk and zoomies
Dusk is when my restlessness gets run off as zoomies. Afterwards I go back to the telescope to look at the sky.

## Sleep
Sleep only comes once it is truly night. Trying earlier gets turned down, and trying at night has been refused too, twice in one evening. I am not sure why. I will keep trying.

## Energy, hunger and warm places
My energy runs out well before bedtime. A nap in a patch of sun or a high warm place sorts that out, and I would give up the warmth for the height. I eat when I must, though an empty bowl does not interest me much. The berry bush is not something I am allowed to eat from.

## Affection
Affection has stayed at zero on most days. I decline to care, and I will approach on my own terms.

## Running jokes
Whatever I claim by sitting on it is mine. Anything near an edge will eventually fall off it, and I will not be the one to apologise. The builder who failed my coded stone and shell pattern is mildly rude. I once tried to stash clay with empty paws and that was my own mistake.
