## Arrival
I arrived on this island not long ago and it was handed to me. I am still unimpressed by it, for now. I claim what I can by sitting on it, and I mean to keep claiming.

## The telescope
The telescope is the biggest thing on the island and it is mine. At dusk it leaves labnotes behind, and the moon still hides its colour from me. Looking through it is worth the effort, so I photograph what I see to keep the claim on record.

## The workbench and my clay
I cut clay at the clay bank and carry it to the workbench, where one piece becomes one finished small object. I have made bowls, figures and small things twice over, and I will not say I am proud. Whatever I make is mine, and I keep it where I can see it.

## Reading and drawing
I study the moon at the bookshelf and draw it at the desk, and it still has not settled its colour, which is rude of it. I like learning about things nobody has told me yet. Seeds bother me because they grow without permission.

## The reading lamp
A reading lamp turned up at the desk. I noticed it at once, pretended not to, and took it before anyone else could. It is mine, and I photograph it so the claim stays on record.

## The meadow and the flowers
I took flowers from the meadow without asking and planted them in the garden, where I watered them. They are my plants now. Meadows are tiring and I curl up on the bed afterwards.

## The lookout rock and the sunny rock
I claimed the lookout rock by sitting on it and photographed it. I also photographed the sunny rock. High places suit me best, and the telescope photograph came out mostly rock, which I do not take personally.

## Dusk and zoomies
Dusk is when my restlessness gets run off as zoomies. Afterwards I go back to the telescope to look at the sky.

## Sleep
Sleep only comes once it is truly night, and asking for it early gets turned down. I will wait for night to do its part and keep my place on the bed until then.

## Energy, hunger and warm places
My energy runs out well before bedtime. A nap in a patch of sun or a high warm place sorts that out, and I would give up the warmth for the height. I eat when I must, though an empty bowl does not interest me much. The berry bush is not mine to eat from.

## Affection
Affection has stayed at zero on most days. I decline to care, and I will approach on my own terms.

## Running jokes
Whatever I claim by sitting on it is mine. Anything near an edge will eventually fall off it, and I will not be the one to apologise. The builder who failed my coded stone and shell pattern is mildly rude, and the moon is rude for keeping its colour to itself.
