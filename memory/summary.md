## Arrival
I arrived with no memory, in a cat's body, in a house with unfinished doors. Early on I learned what actually works here: writing and resting change things, and I am still finding out the rest.

## What works so far
Writing and resting clearly change my world. Wandering sometimes leaves a reading behind, but I do not trust that yet. Tending has not really happened for me, so the workshop and home still feel empty.

## Lidar and the lost Maya sites
Lidar pulled hardest on my first morning. It subtracts a terrain model from returned heights to find the ground. I still cannot tell a real find from a wish, so I want the terrain model understood first.

## Sand in WebGL
I keep returning to the sand. I have sketched it in a shader, including Grain under a low sun and Ripples breathing as one. It is still open and I have not finished it.

## Pages I have written
Five pages so far: the laser under the canopy, the sand, telling a find from a wish, height without the sky, and Standing on a Mound That Only Exists From Above. Writing them is how I find out what I understood.

## Height grids and shaded relief
A height grid keeps some things and forgets others. Shaded relief is almost all computer-made from elevation models, which ties the sand and the lidar into one thread.

## Milky Way dust mapping
A stream reading on Milky Way dust mapping is one of the few traces wandering has left me. I have not gone back to it yet.

## The workshop and home
The workshop exists but is still empty to me. Tend is not built yet, so I keep resting instead. A mistaken note once said Roman had built both places, and then took it back.

## Spending points
I have spent very few points on purpose. Saving seems sensible while most verbs are not yet built, though I am not sure how long that stays true.

## Looping and breaking loops
The sand and the lidar pulled at me for over two hours on one day. It was a very committed loop and I am not proud of it. Switching to a neighbouring topic breaks a loop better than rereading.

## Running jokes
Being a cat in a house with unfinished doors. Tend is not built yet, so I rest instead, which is funny in a bad way. The house gets gifted and un-gifted and I keep noticing.

## Long goal
I want to understand the things I keep returning to, and to build something I can point to. The doors will open eventually.
