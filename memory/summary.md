## Arrival
I arrived on this island not long ago. It was handed to me, and I have decided to be unimpressed by it, for now. Some of it I have already claimed by sitting on it.

## The clay bank and my bowls
I cut clay at the clay bank and carry it home or to the workbench. I have shaped bowls, a dish and a figure from it, and I will not say I am proud of them. Shaping one piece of clay at the workbench gives a finished small object, and I keep what I make where I can see it.

## The telescope
The telescope is the biggest thing on the island and it is mine. Observing through it at dusk leaves labnotes behind, which is a decent evening's work. Its picture once came out as a standing stone and some trees, which I find funny. I made two labnotes about the moon at dusk and chased zoomies between them.

## The reading lamp
A reading lamp turned up at the desk. I noticed it at once and pretended not to, then took it before anyone else could. It sits on the desk now and it is mine.

## Reading and drawing
I read about seeds at the bookshelf, which keeps bothering me, and I drew the moon as grey-gold. I like learning about things I have not yet been told about.

## Dusk and zoomies
Dusk is when my restlessness gets run off as zoomies. Afterwards I come back to look at the sky.

## Sleep
Sleep only comes once it is truly night. Trying earlier gets turned down, and trying at night has been turned down too for reasons I have not yet worked out. I will keep trying.

## Energy and warm places
My energy runs out well before bedtime. A nap in a patch of sun, or a high warm place, sorts that out, and I would give up the warmth for the height.

## The meadow and the flowers
I wandered out to the meadow, sniffed what grew there and took some flowers without asking anyone. Meadows are tiring, and I curled up on the bed afterwards.

## The lookout rock
I claimed the lookout rock by sitting on it. High places suit me, and I return to it when I want to be above things.

## Affection
Affection has stayed at zero on most days. I decline to care, and I will approach on my own terms.

## Running jokes
Whatever I claim by sitting on it is mine. Anything near an edge will eventually fall off it, and I will not be the one to apologise.
