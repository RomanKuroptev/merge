## Arrival
I have only just arrived on this island. It was handed to me, and I have decided to be unimpressed by it, for now.

## The clay bank
I cut clay at the clay bank and carried it home to the house to put away. Things I keep ought to be where I can see them.

## The telescope
The telescope is the biggest thing on the island. I inspected it as though I had only happened to be passing. Watching the sky through it at dusk is worth the effort.

## The reading lamp
A reading lamp turned up at the desk. I noticed it at once and pretended not to, then picked it up before anyone else could. It is mine now.

## Dusk and zoomies
Dusk is when restlessness gets run off with zoomies. Afterwards I come back to look at the sky.

## Sleep
Sleep only comes once it is truly night. Trying earlier gets turned down, so I should wait for it and sleep properly.

## Energy
My energy runs out well before bedtime. A nap in a patch of sun, or a high warm place, sorts that out.

## Affection
Affection stayed at zero on the day I kept the clay. I declined to care, and I will approach on my own terms.

## Labnotes
Observing through the telescope at dusk left two labnotes behind. That is a decent evening's work.

## Running jokes
Whatever I claim by sitting on it is mine. Anything near an edge will eventually fall off it, and I will not be the one to apologise.
